Pawmate

Concept Creator & Lead Designer

Pawmate is an app designed to help users find their perfect dog breed with ease. Beyond the match, users can connect with others through Yappy — a social space to chat, share, and celebrate their dog matches. The app also includes guides to help users discover the best products and routines for them and their pup, bringing the dog lover community together in one place.

This was a group project at UCF with a team of five. I came up with the original concept and contributed heavily to the design work — including the main logo, app background, button styles, spacing, and overall layout decisions.

Purrfect Pear Cat Cafe

Developed with Claude

Purrfect Pear is a pear-themed cat cafe website where users can browse cats, reserve tables, and use a menu system. Data is saved using localStorage, and the site is designed to be clean and responsive — built with Claude.

If I were to improve this project further, I would add more images and icons to sections like the menu to make the design feel more complete. I would also expand the number of cats featured on the site. If this were a real project, I would partner with a local shelter to include real adoptable cats.
